Our direction

What we're building toward.

Built for how people work

Explore interfaces that fit how people use computers today and how they could use them tomorrow.

Simple by default

Everyday use should be straightforward, with a terminal and deeper control available when you want them.

Updates you can trust

System updates should be atomic, reversible, and tested before they reach you.

Build on what works

Use existing software where it fits. Build new software where something is missing.

Free base OS, room to customize

The base OS should always be free of charge and open to personal modification. As AI makes tailored software easier to build, the OS should make room for your own tools and workflows.

A sustainable project

Full-time development needs funding. We're experimenting with ways to support it, which could include optional paid services. ShapeBit is source-available under the PolyForm Shield License; we're open to other approaches and suggestions.
