Made for everyone

No terminal required.
Always there if you want it.

ShapeBit is for people who just want their computer to work. Sensible defaults and clear settings cover everyday life, and the terminal and deeper controls wait quietly for when you get curious.

Workspace

Built for how people work.

Files, terminal and music laid out side by side, so you spend your time working instead of dragging windows around. Designed for how people use computers today, and ready for how they will tomorrow.

Customization

Shape it around you.

Free to use and free to change. Pick your colors and layout, add the apps you like, and build your own tools when you're ready. ShapeBit makes room for them.

Stability

Stable by design.

ShapeBit stands on Fedora, one of the most trusted foundations in Linux. Updates never break your day, and if something doesn't feel right, you step back to the version that did.

  • Fedora foundation
  • Atomic updates, tested before release
  • One-step rollback
