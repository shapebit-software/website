Window management

Windows that arrange themselves.

No more dragging and resizing. Pick from eight tiling layouts and switch whenever you like.

Offline dictation

Talk instead of type.

Built-in speech-to-text runs entirely on your computer. Dictate notes, messages and documents with no internet connection, and nothing you say is ever sent to the cloud.

AI agent

Private by default. Powerful when you choose.

Ask in plain words and the agent gets it done on your computer. Out of the box it runs fully offline, so your files and conversations stay on your device.

Want more power? Online AI models are on the way, as an option you switch on.

Customizable desktop

Reshape the whole shell.

Not just colours and wallpapers. Choose where the bar lives, what's on it and how it looks. Add, remove and rearrange buttons and indicators until the desktop works the way you think.

Automatic updates

Updates that take care of themselves.

ShapeBit stands on Fedora, one of the most trusted foundations in Linux. Updates install automatically in the background, atomic and tested before release. If something doesn't feel right, you step back to the version that did.

Easy to use

No terminal required. Always there if you want it.

ShapeBit is for people who just want their computer to work. Sensible defaults and clear settings cover everyday life, and the terminal and deeper controls wait quietly for when you get curious.

Pricing

Free. Now and always.

ShapeBit OS is free to download, use and change, and it stays that way. To fund full-time development, we're exploring optional paid online services that add real value on top, possibly backups.

You'll never need them to use your computer.
